# K-000003 — Relationship access and transferable buyer trust

**State:** provisional semantic interpretation, not a general causal law. **Origin:** PRJ-0002, 11 November 2026. Workspace counter allocated before creation.

Introductions can give a young vendor access to cooperative customers and product feedback while leaving open how it earns trust from buyers outside those relationships. This distinction is especially relevant where software influences consequential evaluations and founder support is part of delivery.

RedyHire's founder credits organizational introductions and customer feedback, then reports difficulty convincing unfamiliar buyers after shifting attention toward growth. That supports the distinction as a conditional reading of his experience, not a quantified explanation of sales. Price, service effort, product efficacy and customer demand remain alternatives.
