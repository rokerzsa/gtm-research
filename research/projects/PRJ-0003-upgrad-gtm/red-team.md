## RT-0001 adjudication — support is a proposition, not a proven treatment effect

Distinct discovery: searched refund/consumer-commission/loan/misleading-claim and independent outcome/salary/completion questions after freezing. Inspected new SRC-0035–0038, institutional SRC-0059/0061/0067, and SRC-0076/0078/0079. Sought official judgment recovery; accessible full reproduction was retained with original/appeal limits. Identical pathway complaint narratives in several subreddits were treated as one lineage. Unrelated coaching penalties were not assigned to UpGrad. Search also found a branded 2026 AI survey on upgradrise.com, but publisher relationship/publication-before-cutoff was unverified; it is excluded from company findings.

Strongest objection: money committed at enrollment can be hard to recover even if the learner cannot continue or achieve the intended transition; support promises therefore cannot establish customer success. The reproduced consumer order has more evidentiary weight on a specific deposit dispute than anonymous claims, but neither estimates population incidence. Public program terms independently establish the boundary between provider, lender and institution.

Counterweight: the university-sector case, early learner report and university contract describe concrete supported learning, not just an invented service story. Interested case studies still lack selection controls, full response denominators and comparable nonparticipants. The career report is partly labor-market forecasting, not an outcome evaluation; the handbook's survey is self-reported.

Disposition: **qualified**. CLM-0005/0011/0013/0019/0020 may support a premium, assisted learning and local-support design. Revise any implication of universally delivered high quality, guaranteed jobs, causal salary uplift or proven improved retention. Confidence stays medium on the mechanism; narrow artifact readings remain high. No adverse complaint is generalized into systemwide fraud. A dated independent cohort audit, delivered-service records and appropriately matched outcomes would resolve the causal question; more duplicate testimonials/complaints will not. Carry GAP-0002 into qualified synthesis, preserving these limits beside the affected argument.
