# The complete GTM system

UpGrad's common commercial theme is helping people or organizations move toward a desired capability or career result. The work required changes with the purchaser. An individual wants a credible next step; an employer wants employees or candidates capable of doing particular work; a university wants teaching, technology or access to students. This chapter connects the full cycle across those relationships. Later chapters investigate the consequential choices behind it.

## Customers, users and payment units

| Commercial relationship | Who uses and chooses | What is purchased and how money enters |
|---|---|---|
| Professional programs, management learning and technical certification | Working adults; employers may also sponsor learning | A course, cohort or credential-linked commitment; includes KnowledgeHut, Talentedge, INSOFE and Harappa roles, with different program terms |
| Enterprise learning and talent services | HR, learning and business stakeholders commission work used by employees or hiring teams | Customized training, workforce services or recruitment engagements; public contract prices generally unavailable |
| University/campus services | Institutions choose services; students receive teaching or classroom support | At least one historical university contract charged per enrolled student; other institutional economics are not public |
| Undergraduate and campus-linked study | Students/families evaluate; universities retain specified academic decisions | Degree tuition and screening/service payments differ; the branded School of Technology does not collect university tuition |
| Study abroad pathways and recruitment | Students evaluate a study destination; institutions and recruitment partners participate | Pathway learning differs from GSP (Global Study Partners), whose recruitment platform is free to students; tuition collection is not uniformly UpGrad revenue |
| Internshala and Rekrut | Students/candidates and hiring employers | Learning offers, employer tools or recruitment/staffing services; separate transactions and counterparties |
| Unacademy and PrepLadder | Exam candidates choose goal-specific preparation | Subscription or program access around an exam objective |
| Graphy and Airlearn | Creators buy business infrastructure; language learners use interactive lessons | Creator software plans; free-to-paid language features, respectively |

These are product and contractual distinctions, rather than a revenue ranking. Exact current legal consolidation is not established for every institution-linked offer. The group relationship should not be inferred solely from shared branding. Supporting offer examples include [enterprise services](https://www.upgrad-enterprise.com/), [KnowledgeHut](https://www.knowledgehut.com/), [GSP's payment disclosure](https://www.upgradgsp.com/financial-disclosure/), [Graphy](https://graphy.com/us/pricing/) and [Airlearn terms](https://airlearn.com/terms).

## How the commercial model changed

The common promise widened from a particular learning commitment toward support across education and work. That changed the unit being sold, rather than simply enlarging the catalog. These periods overlap: employer capability began early, and later businesses did not replace the professional learner.

| Period | Change in customer work and purchase | Change in reaching and serving that customer |
|---|---|---|
| 2015–16 | Entrepreneurship cohorts and university-associated analytics made structured professional learning purchasable; an employer-training acquihire followed | Concentrated audience discovery and supported cohorts; academic credibility and employer deployment created different relationships |
| 2018–19 | College/community work and foreign university programs extended the career proposition | Institutions and specialist communities added access and delivery responsibilities beyond a direct course sale |
| 2020–22 | Broader advertising accompanied acquisitions in certification, higher education, technical depth, leadership, recruitment and overseas access | Some deals consolidated an existing learning market; others added organizational buyers, networks or physical capabilities; specialist autonomy coexisted with coordination needs |
| 2022–24 | Consolidation sought to coordinate acquired businesses | Shared operations were a stated efficiency route; failed deals, retrenchment and program disruption complicated execution |
| 2025–26 before Unacademy closing | Dedicated local centres, applied-AI offers and early-talent access widened entry points | Face-to-face evaluation, employer partnerships and marketplace interactions supplemented direct digital acquisition |
| September 2026 onward, within the cutoff | Reported Unacademy closing added exam preparation, creator software and language-learning motions | Free entry, subscriptions and software onboarding bring different conversion and repeat-use requirements; integration results remain unestablished |

This comparison draws on the [early employer report](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/deals/-ma/ronnie-screwvalas-ed-tech-startup-upgrad-acquihires-pyoopil/articleshow/54891821.cms), [contemporary portfolio account](https://www.nishithdesai.com/fileadmin/user_upload/pdfs/NDA%20In%20The%20Media/Quotes/upGrad-From-diplomas-to-doctorates,-how-upGrad-scaled-up-continued-learning---The-Economic-Times.pdf), [consolidation plan](https://yourstory.com/2022/11/upgrad-merge-mas-into-parent-company-ipo) and [closing report](https://www.business-standard.com/education/news/unacademy-completes-deal-with-screwvala-s-upgrad-at-200-mn-valuation-126090101254_1.html). Deep dives explain specific choices and counterevidence. No period boundary implies a completed legal merger or successful customer hand-off.

## From need to discovery

For a professional learner, the trigger may be a desired promotion, changing technical requirements or a career transition. UpGrad's 2022 marketing account connected campaigns with appraisal-season dissatisfaction and differentiated MBA, doctorate, coding and overseas ambitions. These are stated targeting choices, not measured explanations of every enrollment. Early entrepreneurship marketing was narrower: a founder described concentrating resources in three major cities, using entrepreneurship media, events and expert interviews. Later television advertising around cricket's Indian Premier League (IPL) sought broader recognition, with digital reinforcement. Incentivized referral terms supply another route into the funnel. [Segmented campaign account](https://www.businessoutreach.in/arjun-mohan-of-upgrad-post-appraisal-season-is-a-great-time-for-us-to-start-talking-to-our-customers/), [early marketing account](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/article/companies/lessons-in-marketing-early-stage-challenges-116010600259_1.html), [IPL account](https://www.exchange4media.com/ipl-news/post-the-ipl-tv-campaign-viewers-started-associating-upgrad-with-specialization-111499.html), [referral terms](https://www.upgrad.com/refer-and-earn/).

Enterprise discovery works differently. Published capabilities invite a discussion about organizational needs and tailored delivery. A consulting partnership with Adaptive SAG provides an advertised route into European business-services buyers. University relationships can put learning inside an existing institution rather than require every student to discover a standalone course. GSP connects institutions and recruitment partners. These routes potentially borrow access and trust from another organization, but their respective shares of originated business are unknown. [Enterprise positioning](https://www.upgrad-enterprise.com/about-us), [consulting counterparty](https://adaptivesag.com/adaptive-solutions-advisory-group-unites-with-upgrad-enterprise-to-guide-business-services-towards-ai-automation-powerhouse/), [GSP](https://www.upgradgsp.com/).

## Evaluation, commitment and monetization

The high-involvement course sale asks the learner to believe several things at once: that the curriculum is relevant, that the credential or institution is credible, that the schedule is manageable and that the expected benefit warrants the fee. Program pages bring these arguments together through curriculum, institutional association, projects, support and career services. Alternatives include lower-cost self-study, direct university study, another training provider and postponing the decision. The historical pricing challenge was explicit even in 2016. [Contemporary scrutiny](https://www.techcircle.in/2016/07/01/why-ronnie-screwvalas-ed-tech-startup-upgrad-faces-an-uphill-climb/).

A selected analytics page associated with the International Institute of Information Technology, Bangalore (IIIT Bangalore), makes commitment concrete: application and aptitude screening lead to an offer, a seat-reservation payment and the balance before the deadline. It displays a ₹3.25 lakh (₹325,000) fee and financing options. These are available terms for that offer, not the group's average realized selling price. Offline-centre pages instead show local counselling, batch choice and a different reservation amount. There is no universal group deposit or purchasing policy. [Analytics admissions](https://www.upgrad.com/data-science-pgd-iiitb-for-tech/), [centre admissions](https://www.upgrad.com/offline-centres/).

Institutional and employer sales require another form of evaluation. A university must coordinate curriculum, teaching responsibilities, student numbers and payment. An enterprise must align the training or staffing scope with the organization's intended result. The public evidence describes tailored programs and one detailed historical university purchase arrangement; it does not reveal a universal procurement cycle, enterprise price list or contract margin. Those unknowns limit any attempt to compare consumer acquisition costs directly with institutional selling costs.

The acquired digital models reduce or redirect the initial commitment. Unacademy offers free entry into an exam-goal environment before paid access. PrepLadder organizes medical exam preparation around videos, questions and revision. Graphy sells creators tools to run their own knowledge businesses. Airlearn offers language exercises with selected free access and paid features. An exam candidate buys preparation; a creator buys operating infrastructure. Treating all four as leads for a premium degree would erase their immediate reasons to buy. [Unacademy](https://unacademy.com/), [PrepLadder](https://www.prepladder.com/), [Graphy](https://graphy.com/us/pricing/), [Airlearn](https://airlearn.com/).

The institutional workflow also has a concrete payment relationship: a dated university purchase arrangement specified specializations, student batches, work orders, teaching/support responsibilities and invoicing to the university. The intended learner used the service while the institution bought it; actual collections were not inspected. By contrast, GSP—Global Study Partners—facilitates international recruitment while its disclosure directs tuition payments to the educational institution. Exact recruitment commissions remain unknown. These are different routes to money, even if both involve university students. [University contract](https://sushantuniversity.edu.in/admin-assets/uploaddata/sunaac/sunaacqtm/651sunaacqtm3a.pdf), [GSP payment allocation](https://www.upgradgsp.com/financial-disclosure/).

For employers, talent fulfillment includes supplier sourcing/screening and employer final interviews, followed by the relevant permanent or contingent service. Training and deployment are also advertised together, without evidence that every hire follows that sequence. Creator software instead uses a trial/demo, publishing/payment tools and migration assistance to help a customer operate its own business. Its ongoing usefulness differs from finishing a cohort. [Talent workflow](https://www.upgrad-enterprise.com/talent-fulfillment), [Graphy offer](https://graphy.com/us/pricing/).

## Onboarding, delivery and useful adoption

For supported courses, the fee buys a period of organized learning rather than an unlimited promise of employment. Content, assignments, expert interaction and support help turn intention into progress. University partners may control admission or academic awards; the service provider coordinates other parts of the experience. Historical offers already included mentors and success management. The support model consequently creates staffing, scheduling and quality-control obligations alongside the digital content. [Historical entrepreneurship offer](https://staticweb.upgrad.com/entrepreneurship-certificate-program/?iref=Footer_Program_Entrepreneurship), [university-sector partnership account](https://www.universitiesuk.ac.uk/sites/default/files/uploads/UUKi%20reports/the-scale-of-UK-HE-TNE-2018-19.pdf).

Other models define adoption differently. Employer learning must be used in work; creator software must help a creator publish, sell or manage customers; recruitment must produce useful candidate-employer interactions. Internshala's employer workflow includes registration, application access, shortlisting and interviews, with paid advanced capabilities. Rekrut separately advertises recruitment, staffing and payroll/compliance services. A job platform connection is therefore not evidence that every candidate was trained by UpGrad. [Internshala hiring guide](https://internshala.com/blog/employer-how-to-hire-on-internshala/), [Rekrut](https://upgrad-rekrut.com/).

## Recurrence, feedback and the evolving system

Net revenue retention measures how revenue from an existing customer base changes after expansion, contraction and lost accounts. It differs from a count or share of customers who buy again. The available enterprise repeat-client statement supports the narrower description only.

Recurrence can mean a second course, a referred learner, another corporate engagement or a software renewal. These should be measured separately. Company reporting described more than 80% repeat clients in its enterprise portfolio, but that is not a disclosed net revenue retention calculation. Referral terms reward a valid fully paid enrollment after cohort commencement, including referrals by registered nonstudents. They establish an incentive mechanism, not unpaid advocacy or the share of demand it produces. [FY25 disclosure reporting](https://entrackr.com/fintrackr/upgrad-turns-ebitda-positive-in-fy25-reports-rs-1943-cr-gross-revenue-10605459), [referral eligibility](https://www.upgrad.com/refer-and-earn/).

Feedback is visible in early events and learner accounts; institutional partnerships and tailored enterprise work also create opportunities for adjustment. Public material does not establish a group-wide process connecting feedback to curriculum or sales decisions. Nor do selected testimonials establish population completion, repeat purchase or causal salary gains. The full commercial cycle is observable as a design, with substantial gaps in its measured effectiveness.

The main bottlenecks differ across this system. A learner must progress after making a financial commitment; an institution must coordinate academic and service responsibilities; an employer must use the capability it commissioned; a marketplace must create useful matches; software must remain useful enough to retain paid access. A failure at one step can weaken the reason to buy the next service. This is a conditional explanation of how models connect, rather than measured evidence of a group-wide retention loop.

UpGrad's broader positioning therefore has to work at two levels: the group can promise help across learning and work, while each offer must justify its own purchase. Historical expansion assembled more offers and relationships. Whether they reinforce one another economically remains the unresolved test.
