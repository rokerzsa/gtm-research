# Evidence notes and reading limits

This book draws on 89 registered source records, not 89 independent confirmations. Records include overlapping company communications, interested counterparties, journalism, terms, original institutional artifacts and an unavailable financial-statement lead. Retrieval used public/free material without outreach, paid access, registration or live product testing. The event cutoff is 11 November 2026; some retrieval and review occurred on 12 November.

## Origins and outcomes

The September 2015 launch/application announcement and November teaching-start recollection describe different events. The founder blog was republished or updated later, so it is not treated as a contemporary invoice ledger. A January 2016 founder column is stronger evidence of early marketing execution than later retrospective generalizations. The named learner in contemporary journalism is not established as the first payer.

The IIIT Bangalore annual report was downloaded from the institution, rendered and visually checked at printed p.86, PDF p.88. Its table separates responses, applications, enrollments and learners still studying. OCR was cross-checked against the page. No final completion or cash-collection rate is inferred. The Liverpool John Moores partnership account comes from a university-sector publication with interested contributors, rather than an independent causal evaluation. Career-report forecasts and handbook survey responses likewise do not establish the effect of studying compared with not studying.

## Institutional roles and portfolio boundaries

The Sushant University compilation was inspected at PDF pp.145–149, including the poor/blank page. Scanned contract pages were rendered and visually read; failed OCR was not used to guess clauses. The amendment establishes dated contractual terms, not performance or today's price. Uninspected master agreements and work orders remain outside the conclusion.

School of Technology policies explicitly allocate degree and tuition responsibility to universities. ATLAS institutional reports establish activity and relationships; the unavailable financial mirror does not establish current accounting control. The Singapore institute's registration description comes from its brochure, not a directly inspected regulator entry. Vanuatu sources document a 2023 launch/enrollment initiative, with later operating scale unverified. None establishes universal recognition or professional licensing eligibility.

The GATE Academy announcement contradicts an unqualified first-ever 2026 test-prep entry. Exampur's later failure supersedes an earlier portfolio account. Current party submissions and older transaction announcements cannot be reconciled into a continuous legal ownership history without further records. The book preserves this boundary.

For Unacademy, party submissions describe the proposed combination; CCI's notice establishes approval; closing reporting and the buyer's legal advisor support completion. The detailed regulator order and individual brand schedules were not recovered. Pre-close brand reporting plus whole-group closing reporting informs the commercial map, with exact legal schedules qualified. The book makes no claim about realized integration results.

## Friction and financial interpretation

INSOFE protest reporting and the company response must be read together: a discontinued program is acknowledged, city-wide closure is denied, and loan remedies are promised rather than verified complete. The reports share the initial protest lineage. Neither establishes a final judgment, group-wide incidence or complete closure. A 2022 UGDX campus target likewise remains a plan, separate from current institutional branding and later support centres. Harappa's consumer-course brochure has 2022 admissions dates; its survival online does not establish a currently available cohort.

Program terms show disclosed responsibilities and restrictions, not compliance in every transaction or legal advice. The consumer order was read through a secondary judgment repository; its official signed copy, appeal and compliance status were not recovered. An anonymous complaint repeated across forums is one unverified narrative. The book uses these to test the supported-learning proposition, rather than to estimate misconduct prevalence.

FY24–26 company financial communications are distinguished from filing-based reporting. The latter is still secondhand access to underlying accounts. The latest FY26 article was inspected only in its accessible synopsis/opening, rather than the full cost tables. Critical derivative coverage and financial commentary prompted challenges but were not elevated into original audit evidence. Gross revenue, recognized operating revenue, total income, EBITDA, annual net loss and cash flow remain separate quantities. FY26 predates the Unacademy closing.

## Review and current status

Four substantive adversarial source-seeking reviews were performed after the initial propositions were frozen: premium support and outcomes; acquisition channels; portfolio/integration; and financial/AI claims. The same investigator conducted distinct passes. Final coverage, depth, evidence and comprehension reviews were also performed against the manuscript, including a return to primary evidence to repair the employer workflow. This is not an independent external audit or reader test. The book is ready for user review with the stated qualifications; user acceptance remains pending.

Direct public links beside material claims provide the reader's source path. Detailed access states, claim confidence, review adjudications and gap investigations are retained in the project records outside the book. New evidence can change the interpretation, especially measured outcomes, audited financial bridges and post-close customer results.
