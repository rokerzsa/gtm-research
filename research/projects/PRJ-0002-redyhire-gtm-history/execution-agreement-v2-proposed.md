## Strategic questions and complete GTM coverage

- What problem did the original product solve, for whom, and against which alternatives? What did the workflow actually do?
- How did entity history, founder involvement, early product development and launch connect?
- Who were the earliest substantiated users and paying accounts? How did they discover, evaluate, purchase, onboard and use the offer?
- Who was the user, champion, hiring manager, economic buyer and budget owner? How did the product fit their workflow?
- How did awareness, acquisition, evaluation, conversion, pricing, monetization, service/delivery, support, retention, expansion and referrals operate and change?
- Which channels produced customers, which were experiments, and which had visible activity without attributable outcomes?
- How did product scope, positioning, target segments, sales process, trial/packaging and geographic reach change? What alternatives were considered, what trade-offs followed, and what evidence connects execution with outcomes?
- What failed or stalled? What constrained growth? What changed in response?
- What can be established about customer scale, funding, revenue quality, retention, hiring outcomes and economics?
- Which explanations survive a separate adversarial source investigation, alternative causal accounts and explicit falsification conditions?

Test competing explanations including founder relationships, customer co-development, service effort, market timing, pricing, product breadth, reputation and competitor changes. Separate stated strategy, observed execution and demonstrated results.
