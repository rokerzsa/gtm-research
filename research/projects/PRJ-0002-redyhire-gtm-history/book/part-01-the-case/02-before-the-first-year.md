# Before the first year

RedyHire's earliest visible history does not begin with a clean launch announcement. A secondary company registry reports RedyHire Technologies LLP incorporated on 15 July 2019. An official government startup list whose filename is dated 2023 includes the same named entity. These establish an older organizational trace, with different levels of authority; neither establishes when an employer first paid for software. The registry's ₹1 lakh contribution figure is capital information, not sales or venture funding. [Registry profile](https://www.tofler.in/redyhire-technologies-llp/company/AAP-9166), [government list](https://www.startupindia.gov.in/content/dam/invest-india/Templates/public/ListofStartups_03112023.pdf).

## The earlier product proposition

The stronger product artifact is a homepage captured on 8 November 2020. It describes research-backed assessments, a cloud platform and analytics intended to improve recruiting decisions. The advertised uses extend from pre-hiring assessment to employee and student skills, certification and contests. A May 2022 capture contains the same assessment text and matching archive digest. This proves earlier public positioning and persistence of the page, not two independent demonstrations of an operating business. [2020 capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20201108085542/https://redyhire.com/), [2022 capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20220516114414/https://redyhire.com/).

The original commercial idea was thus wider than a single coding test. Assessment could be reused wherever an organization needed evidence of skill. That breadth offered several possible markets, but the archive does not reveal which one purchased, how a sale worked or whether any of those uses became material. The positioning is evidence of ambition rather than market validation.

A September 2024 capture recovers only a minimal page shell. It cannot establish an outage, a product reset or the absence of features. Attempts to inspect older product, company and FAQ child pages returned unavailable captures. The connection between the early website and the more recent venture remains open: continuous development, a dormant prototype, changed operators or a later restart are possible explanations. No inspected evidence selects among them. [September 2024 capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20240925034832/https://redyhire.com/).

This matters because the founder's first-anniversary account, published on 5 March 2025 in Indian time, concerns his entrepreneurial journey and a family living-room office. It is a useful account of how he describes building the venture. Reading it as proof that RedyHire first existed in 2024 would erase the earlier artifacts. A directory's 2024 founding label is not sufficient to resolve that discrepancy. [Founder account](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/aditya-kapoor-innovate_gratitude-redyhireturns1-entrepreneurship-activity-7302781170411257883-uvkQ).

## What can be established about early users?

A Zensar hiring post published on 17 October 2024 contains a RedyHire assessment preview/link for a DevOps architect role. This is a useful external operational trace: RedyHire was associated with an actual named hiring context. It does not show a contract, cash receipt or whether Zensar was the earliest user. [Zensar hiring post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/savinderpuri_redyhire-assessment-activity-7252527039641960448--p0a).

The founder's 7 November 2024 pitch describes AI skills assessment for technical and nontechnical hiring. He reported helping a customer hire hundreds of prompt engineers within twenty days and invited a personal demonstration and free trial. In the later anniversary account, the project is described as 200 prompt engineers for an unnamed IT services customer. These are two versions from one interested source, not independent confirmation. The customer identity, baseline process, meaning of a completed hire, fee and software's causal contribution remain unverified. [November pitch](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/aditya-kapoor-innovate_innovation-success-failfast-activity-7260195624615096320-LHnV), [anniversary account](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/aditya-kapoor-innovate_gratitude-redyhireturns1-entrepreneurship-activity-7302781170411257883-uvkQ).

As a sales proposition, that example is understandable. A high-volume, time-sensitive requirement makes manual evaluation visibly costly. A tool that helps process applicants and produces useful screening evidence has a concrete job to do. But a dramatic project can also depend on unusual demand, recruiters' labor, relaxed constraints or an existing relationship. Without the customer's own account, the example cannot identify which factor drove the reported result or whether it generalized to ordinary hiring.

The distinction between use and payment remains essential. A Trustt vacancy hosted on RedyHire carries an August 2025 date; a later Sahi customer comment supplies another association. These add substance to a customer narrative without identifying the first paying account. An available job page also differs from an immutable contemporaneous capture: its displayed date is evidence supplied by the live system. [Trustt vacancy](https://careers.redyhire.com/careers/6853040342125406862/f7dfc5f9-1e60-4920-809b-50ebe9d141da), [Sahi comment on the founder's office post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/aditya-kapoor-innovate_entrepreneurship-zerotoone-bootstrapped-activity-7452543917608280064-jD7n).

## The first commercial mechanism we can describe

The early evidence points toward demonstrating a specific evaluation benefit to an accessible employer, rather than a demonstrated self-serve funnel. A buyer with an urgent screening problem can try a tool on a defined role. The founder can observe the work, answer objections and adapt the product. This is an interpretation of the personal trial pitch and customer-feedback account, not a reconstructed sales transcript.

What the evidence does not provide is equally consequential: the first contract, who approved the budget, the original price, how long evaluation took, or whether the early user renewed. The first substantiated named usage trace in this study is therefore an evidentiary anchor, not a first-customer trophy. The next chapter follows the stronger public explanation of how those relationships were built—and why that explanation became insufficient for the founder's next growth task.
