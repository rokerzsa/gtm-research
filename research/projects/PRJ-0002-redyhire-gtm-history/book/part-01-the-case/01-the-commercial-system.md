# The complete commercial system

RedyHire's commercial proposition begins with a separation of work. Recruiters need to move applicants through a process; managers need credible evidence about ability; candidates need a way to demonstrate it. Software can reduce the work between those parties, but an employer still has to decide whether the resulting evidence deserves trust. The current product presents a connected workflow spanning sourcing, screening, assessment, interviewing and decisions. This chapter maps that whole system before examining its history. [Current offer](https://www.redyhire.com/).

## Who has a reason to buy?

The strongest fit suggested by the public material is an employer or recruiting provider processing enough applicants for repeated screening and preliminary interviews to become expensive. That is an analytical inference from the workflow, not a verified segmentation of revenue. Technical hiring is prominent, but the offer also covers communication and other nontechnical evaluation. A recruiter can operate the system, a hiring manager can define competencies and review results, and an HR or business leader may sponsor purchase. The actual budget owner, approval chain and procurement requirements are not established publicly. [Evaluation scope](https://www.redyhire.com/platform/candidate-evaluation).

The immediate alternative is familiar work: resume review, recruiter calls, manager screening and separate tests, scheduling and communication tools. A buyer might retain those methods, assemble several specialist products or choose an incumbent platform. RedyHire therefore has to justify both the quality of its evaluation and the effort of adopting another system. Cheap automation that generates questionable shortlists can move work downstream rather than remove it; the commercial benefit depends on usable evidence and appropriate human review.

## How the product enters the work

The documented process starts with a job and its criteria. For an AI interview, a recruiter supplies a title or job description, reviews the proposed topics and scoring, and saves the plan. Candidates can then complete an AI-led preliminary interview without coordinating a human interviewer's calendar. Recruiters and managers receive structured material to review before later decisions. Job administration, scheduling, approvals, pre-offer documents and offers sit around that evaluation activity. Human judgment and organizational configuration remain part of the process. [Interview setup](https://www.redyhire.com/help/how-to-create-an-ai-interview), [workflow FAQ](https://ai.redyhire.com/help/faqs), [AI interviews](https://www.redyhire.com/platform/aiinterview).

This makes onboarding a commercial issue. The customer has to translate a vacancy into defensible criteria, decide what candidates may do, configure the evaluation and learn how to interpret the output. The documentation demonstrates those tasks, but it does not measure implementation time, integration reliability, candidate completion or trial conversion. Public product pages describe the intended service; this study did not run a live hiring process.

## How an employer becomes a customer

The early visible selling motion was personal. In a November 2024 post, founder Aditya Kapoor invited prospects to a demonstration and free trial of AI skills assessment. Later, he credited friends making introductions inside organizations and customers suggesting product changes. This connects access, evaluation and product learning, while leaving the actual contract negotiation and payment process unknown. [Early pitch](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/aditya-kapoor-innovate_innovation-success-failfast-activity-7260195624615096320-LHnV), [anniversary account](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/aditya-kapoor-innovate_gratitude-redyhireturns1-entrepreneurship-activity-7302781170411257883-uvkQ).

At the cutoff, the website invites demonstrations, while indexed employee outreach promotes use cases such as campus hiring. Neither visible promotional activity nor a demo button establishes acquisition volume. The founder's April 2026 statement that earlier customers came through referrals is informative about his experience, but there is no independent funnel connecting impressions, leads, pilots, signed contracts and renewals. Referral-led entry and experimentation with broader outreach can coexist. A lack of published conversion data cannot establish that later sales efforts failed. [Growth account](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/aditya-kapoor-innovate_startupjourney-growthchallenges-hiringtech-activity-7448283897706180609-0957).

## What the customer pays for

The official rate card combines a platform subscription with metered evaluation. At inspection, the Indian-rupee plan listed **₹1,500 per user per month**, charged according to the highest daily active-user count during the billing month. A user's allowance included ten active jobs and 1,500 resume screens. Additional active jobs were ₹200, including 100 resumes. AI assessments were ₹200 each, non-AI assessments ₹50 and psychometric assessments ₹200. Conversational AI interviews of 30 minutes were ₹500; coding interviews were ₹750 for 30 minutes and ₹1,500 for 60 minutes. Custom plans were available. These are published terms, not verified invoices or historical prices. [Official pricing](https://ai.redyhire.com/pricing).

The model links payment to two different kinds of use: people operating the platform and candidates consuming evaluation services. More seats, more jobs or more interviews can therefore create expansion revenue in principle. That is a property of the rate card, not evidence of expansion actually occurring. The public record does not establish negotiated discounts, realized revenue, infrastructure costs or gross margin.

For illustration, two billable users plus twenty 30-minute conversational interviews would total ₹13,000 at those listed rates, before other charges or contractual adjustments. This calculation shows why applicant volume matters more than the seat headline alone. It is not a typical customer bill. A directory's differing ₹1,250 seat price lacks a verified effective date, so it cannot establish a price increase or reconstruct an earlier contract. [Directory comparison](https://sourceforge.net/software/compare/RedyHire-vs-springhire/).

Keeping the same two users and twenty interviews in the illustration above but choosing 30-minute **coding** interviews would give ₹18,000 at the listed rates: ₹3,000 platform plus ₹15,000 interview usage. The ₹5,000 difference comes from the work consumed, not more users. The highest-daily-user rule also differs from billing average utilization. These mechanics explain the commercial bet: make routine platform adoption accessible, then charge for evaluation activity. They establish neither a typical bill nor a measured margin or net saving in interviewer time.

## Delivery, continued use and other routes

The founder attributes customer persistence to product quality and support. That suggests service effort may have helped turn initial introductions into continuing relationships, but there is no retention cohort, support workload or renewal rate to test the explanation. Published customer stories describe reduced evaluation effort; they are selected vendor-hosted accounts rather than controlled studies. Continuing value would depend on suitable future vacancies, trusted outputs, candidate participation and dependable operation. [Founder account](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/aditya-kapoor-innovate_startupjourney-growthchallenges-hiringtech-activity-7448283897706180609-0957), [customer accounts](https://www.redyhire.com/).

A provider-assisted route is also visible. TeckLeap describes applicants moving through RedyHire-assisted screening and an AI first round before human interviews and HR onboarding. TeckHealth describes healthcare staffing powered by RedyHire. Those artifacts broaden the delivery context beyond an employer buying software directly. They do not establish partner-sourced sales, ownership, revenue sharing or a scaled overseas business. TeckLeap's workforce service pricing belongs to its service offer; it is not proof that RedyHire replaced software fees with placement fees. [Provider workflow](https://teckleap.com/careers), [healthcare association](https://teckhealthsolutions.com/contactus/), [workforce service](https://teckleap.com/workforce-management).

## How the system evolved

The strongest dated strategic moves concern resource allocation. A personal demo/free-trial pitch was visible in November 2024. On 31 March 2026 the founder described handing product/technology responsibility to colleagues; his 10 April post then questioned whether product quality alone could persuade strangers. The 30 June engineering account describes reusing an execution component to reduce custom infrastructure work. These decisions address different constraints—buyer trust, founder attention and engineering capacity. Together they make a plausible explanation for seeking a broader usable workflow, but do not prove that TARA caused growth or that one decision caused the next. [Founder allocation](03-transferring-trust.md#the-organization-changes-before-the-problem-is-solved), [technical allocation](04-from-evaluation-to-orchestration.md#a-documented-technical-bottleneck).
