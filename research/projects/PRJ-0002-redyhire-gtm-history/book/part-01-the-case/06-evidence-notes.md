# Evidence notes

This study was freshly investigated against public/free sources and accessed through 11 November 2026. The earlier local draft supplied leads only. The book distinguishes source testimony from verified historical events and the author's strategic interpretation. Describing a plausible mechanism does not mean its causal effect has been measured.

## Independence and failed avenues

Founder posts, engineering articles and official help pages are primary evidence of what their authors said or documented. Vendor-hosted customer accounts are selected testimonials. Customer-origin posts provide narrower external traces, while staffing providers have a commercial interest in their own descriptions. Directory pages frequently repeat vendor text. SourcrLab explicitly states that it has not used RedyHire in live recruitment, so its profile cannot independently validate outcomes. [Disclosure](https://sourcrlab.com/tools/redyhire).

Some LinkedIn and directory pages were available only through indexed excerpts; the book identifies consequential uses of those excerpts. Direct-opening cache misses, unavailable archived child pages and an official registry search timeout were retained in the working records. No inaccessible page was treated as inspected. Unproductive searches for independent cases or financial figures establish the limits of this investigation, not proof that such evidence does not exist.

## Review boundary

Major historical, commercial and aggregate explanations received separate adversarial source-seeking passes by the same investigator. These recovered the earlier archives, challenged the apparent product-pivot chronology, checked incumbent integrity capabilities, distinguished service and software pricing, and excluded a client's ARR from RedyHire's metrics. There was no independent human review, customer interview, live product test or financial audit.

The operational source, claim, gap and review registers remain in the project's Markdown workspace. They support this reader-facing book without becoming site chapters. Review-ready status means the investigation and internal checks were completed with explicit qualifications; it does not mean the user has accepted the findings.
