## Ordering and boundaries

Investigate depth-first: establish the business and identity before inferring first customers; establish account and payment evidence before attributing channel results; establish dated changes before judging pivots. P5 reconciles the whole story rather than treating completed phase prose as independent evidence.

Capture later-phase leads without pursuing a material expansion of the active contract. Later detailed plans are prepared from actual preceding findings and require their own approval. Routine tactical search choices within an approved contract remain autonomous.

Each phase's completion requires its applicable approved contract, registered evidence, required independent reviews, explicit critical-gap disposition, usage reconciliation, and a criterion-by-criterion dossier. Budget exhaustion is not evidence exhaustion.

## Proposed reader-facing book

1. What RedyHire does and where the business began.
2. The first product, users, and commercial traction.
3. First customers, sales, pricing, and payment evidence.
4. How customers were acquired, served, and retained.
5. Product, positioning, and market changes.
6. Failed experiments and growth constraints.
7. What explains the journey: historical lessons, alternatives, and limits.

Part indexes and chapter names may be refined within these boundaries; consequential restructuring or changes in research objective require approval. Book prose will derive from traceable source/claim/concept and review records.

## Risks and change triggers

- The reported 2019 entity and 2024 venture story may require a discontinuity explanation; legal and operational continuity remain unverified.
- Private payment, revenue, renewal, and channel-attribution data may be unavailable. Preserve unknowns and search failures under the charter's qualified-completion criteria.
- LinkedIn relative dates, crawled snippets, vendor testimonials, mirrored domains, and syndicated listings can create false chronology or apparent corroboration.
- Product scope may reflect announcement or positioning before deployment; do not infer realized revenue or outcomes from marketing changes.
- Comparative accuracy, fairness, or hidden-tool detection claims may remain untestable in a public historical study. Live benchmarking is outside scope.
- A consequential entity mismatch, new objective, paid-source need, or inaccessible critical evidence may require an amendment or explicit gap decision.
