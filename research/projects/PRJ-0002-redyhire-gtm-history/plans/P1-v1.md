## Objective and boundaries

Establish what RedyHire was at inception and initial commercialization: the relationship between legal formation and the current venture, founder involvement, the earliest product and customer problem, intended users/buyers, and earliest supportable external usage and commercial signals. Investigate dates from original evidence rather than inheriting the earlier memo's chronology.

The period begins with the earliest relevant origin evidence and ends at an adequately supported first-product/initial-commercialization boundary. Later sources may explain earlier events, but their retrospective nature remains explicit. Later product launches, scale, channels, and platform pivots are deferred to P2–P4 unless needed to distinguish identity or explain the original product.

## Questions and adequate evidence

| Question | Evidence sought | Completion condition |
|---|---|---|
| P1-Q1 — Identity and continuity | Official or registry-derived legal records, company legal terms, contemporaneous identifiers, founder accounts | Distinguish legal creation, operational venture, brand/product launch and possible predecessor activity; retain uncertainty if continuity is not established. |
| P1-Q2 — Founders and initial problem | Dated founder biographies/accounts, early company descriptions, launch material, contemporary coverage | Attribute who did what and the original problem; test retrospective explanations against contemporary evidence. |
| P1-Q3 — First product/workflow and audience | Early product/pricing pages, archived docs, launch/demo material and customer-originated usage | Explain inputs, assessment/output, hiring-team action, user/champion/buyer, and alternatives; label hypothetical buyer roles if unsupported. |
| P1-Q4 — Earliest external/commercial signals | Dated customer/recruiter posts, public deployment traces, customer announcements, accessible payment/contract references | Establish the earliest substantiated observations; do not rank payers or assume paid usage from a logo, link, or testimonial. |
| P1-Q5 — Origin narrative and rivals | Contradictory dates, alternative entity histories, contemporaneous descriptions, independent accounts | Review the major origin/first-product explanation against alternatives and state what evidence could falsify it. |

## Work sequence and source strategy

1. Inspect relevant public legal and company identity evidence; build a timeline of observations and separately reported historical events.
2. Reinspect early product/founder leads and search for contemporary launch, workflow, pricing/trial and role-specific descriptions. Use public archives when useful and legitimately accessible.
3. Seek customer-originated and independent evidence for early usage or commercialization. Trace information lineage rather than counting repeated testimonials as corroboration.
4. Conduct a separate adversarial source search focused on the major identity, launch, original-product and early-traction conclusions; adjudicate conflicts or leave material disputes qualified.
5. Reconcile evidence, gaps, usage and dependencies; produce the P1 completion dossier only after its objective audit. Prepare an evidence-linked origins chapter if sufficient reviewed records exist.

Search tactics and individual sources may adapt inside these questions. No specific source is treated as inspected merely because it is named in the old memo.

## Competing hypotheses

- A 2019 legal entity may represent the same continuous business, a dormant/predecessor business, or an entity later used for a new venture.
- The original offer may have been assessment software, recruiting services supported by software, or a mixed offer.
- Early customer references may be paid deployments, unpaid trials, founder-network design partnerships, or later testimonials describing earlier use.
- Founder retrospective explanations may simplify overlapping experiments; earliest indexed evidence may postdate actual launch.

## Review and records

Register material SRC/CLM/GAP records with directness, access form, event/publication/access dates, lineage, confidence and separate review state. Create K concepts only when meaningful synthesis warrants them. Journal failed recovery and changed hypotheses. Major conclusions trigger independent source-seeking RT records, including evidence challenging the leading explanation; no review verdict is assumed in advance.

The earlier draft receives no inherited evidence confidence. Source dates based only on relative LinkedIn labels remain unverified. Current domains and product pages cannot establish a historical product sequence by themselves.

## Budget, limits and stopping conditions

No numerical source, time, pass or token cap is assigned; no extension allowance exists. Public/free access only. Track meaningful source investigations and review activity from documented actions; report token/time consumption unavailable where not reliably observable. Prior pre-protocol work remains separately labeled with unreconciled usage.

This block is bounded by P1-Q1–Q5 and the source avenues above. Stop discovery for a question when its evidence condition is met and relevant competing explanations have been examined, or when meaningful legitimate public recovery avenues are exhausted with the limitation documented. Repetitive derivative pages do not justify indefinite searching. Record later-phase leads without pursuing them substantively.

Pause dependent claims for a material entity mismatch, irreconcilable consequential dispute, unavailable critical evidence, paid-access need, or new scope. Continue unaffected authorized P1 questions. Neither a source quota nor budget exhaustion establishes evidence exhaustion.

## Outputs and closure

Required: registered evidence and counterevidence, a qualified timeline/product/buyer explanation, question-status matrix, material gaps, applicable independent review, journal and usage ledger, dependencies, and criterion-by-criterion P1 dossier. A chapter is derived only from traceable records and carries uncertainty proportionately.

P1 may finish with explicitly reviewed public-evidence limitations under the charter, but cannot claim a known launch date, continuous entity history, first payer, or successful business model without evidence. A foundational unresolved dispute must be adjudicated, explicitly permitted as a qualified limitation, or escalated before dependent work proceeds.
