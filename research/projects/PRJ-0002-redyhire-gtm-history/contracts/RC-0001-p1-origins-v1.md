## Objective, scope and definitions

Reconstruct RedyHire's origins and initial product/commercialization, establishing the legal-versus-operational timeline, founder roles, first product/workflow, intended user/buyer, and earliest supportable customer and commercial signals. Scope is the approved high-level plan's P1; the [attached detailed plan](../plans/P1-v1.md) defines execution tasks and question labels.

Legal incorporation, brand creation, operational launch, product availability, first user, paying account, contract, invoice and cash receipt are separate events. Public usage is not proof of payment. A vendor account is evidence that the account was reported, with underlying historical truth assessed separately.

Exclude later-stage channel performance, full customer/price histories, current growth recommendations, paid datasets, external contact, private-account access, live product tests and publishing. Later leads may be recorded and deferred. Accessible retrospective sources may illuminate origin events if clearly attributed.

## Investigation and legitimate source recovery

Use publicly accessible official identity records and company legal descriptions; dated founder/company artifacts; contemporary launch/product/pricing/docs material; customer-originated usage and deployment traces; credible independent reporting; and publicly available archive captures. Search by questions and event windows, not to fill a URL quota. Inspect originals when accessible; label snippets, derivative descriptions and failed original recovery honestly.

Seek plausible alternatives: predecessor or dormant entity, a new venture under an older entity, software versus service-led initial offer, unpaid trial versus paying customer, later retrospective storytelling, and launch preceding the earliest public trace. Do not infer a causal growth mechanism from an early successful project alone.

No source is claimed inspected by being named here. Paywalls, login restrictions and access controls remain respected. Material inaccessible-source recovery attempts and their limits enter source/gap/journal records.
