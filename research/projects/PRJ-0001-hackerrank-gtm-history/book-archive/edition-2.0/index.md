# HackerRank: GTM strategy, evolution and current bets

HackerRank connects developer skills with employer decisions. This book explains the complete business system, the strategic choices that changed it, and the current bets around AI-led evaluation, learning and model assessment.

Start with the integrated GTM chapter. The deeper chapters examine decisions and mechanisms rather than treating every launch as a successful pivot. The research runs from early InterviewStreet experiments through November 11, 2026.
