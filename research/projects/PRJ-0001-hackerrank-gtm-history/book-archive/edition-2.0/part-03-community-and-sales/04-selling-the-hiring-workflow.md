# Selling a workflow into an employer account

**The commercial opportunity extended beyond a better coding test:** make evaluation usable inside the employer's recruiting process. That creates potential for recurring use and account expansion, but adds implementation and trust requirements.

[Complete company GTM system](../part-00-gtm-system/00-full-gtm-system.md)

## Enter through an existing bottleneck

An employer may already have candidates and need a better way to decide whom engineers should interview. VMware's published case describes that screening bottleneck, peer recommendations and challenges placed in its own applicant process. It is a selected vendor-hosted account, not representative performance measurement. [1](#note-1)

This route is strategically different from selling access to a developer audience. The customer supplies candidate flow. The offer can therefore be valuable even if the community never generated the employer lead.

## Integration changes adoption work

An applicant tracking system stores candidates and hiring stages. HackerRank's announced integrations let assessment activity fit into systems recruiters already used. Jobvite's partner description corroborates the workflow at inspection, without independently dating its first availability. [2](#note-2)

The plausible benefit is less switching and information transfer. The trade-off is dependence on an employer's configuration, process and integration maintenance. Integration can support continued use; it does not by itself prove higher retention or close a sale.

## An account can expand in several ways

| Possible expansion | Reason it could create value | What is not established |
|---|---|---|
| More candidate activity | Use the existing process for more hiring | Usage-to-revenue relationship across all contracts. |
| More teams or roles | Reuse an adopted supplier and workflow | How widely initial adoption spread. |
| More of the hiring process | Connect screening and live evaluation | Incremental product contribution versus bundling. |
| Adjacent skills work | Apply assessment beyond an immediate hire | Shared budget, cross-sell rate and retained demand. |

These are mechanisms, not a reported expansion curve. The historical selling descriptions and broader employer scope make them reasonable questions; they do not rank their commercial importance. [3](#note-3)

## Broader segments require a broader commercial proposition

Banks, retailers and other employers also hire programmers. The company's 2018 enterprise-investment plans and reported cross-industry customers show an ambition beyond software-product companies. Such buyers may need the product to fit a larger operating environment; a coding contest alone would not describe that purchase fully. [3](#note-3)

The relevant strategic choice was whether to remain a narrow tool for a single hiring task or become useful across a larger process. Broader coverage can create more reasons to use the supplier and more complexity to implement. Actual acquisition, renewal and expansion effects remain unisolated.

The next chapter examines why implementation and changing market demand matter to this model; the [Chakra analysis](../part-04-later-shifts/06-the-ai-transition.md) then asks how automating more evaluation changes the offer.
