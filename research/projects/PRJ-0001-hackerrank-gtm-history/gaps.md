## Gap — PRJ-0001:GAP-0001

**Exact unresolved question:** What was the full early product sequence—from the mentor-matching service recalled in 2018, through the 2009 mock-interview service, later master's-admissions assistance and college-feedback iterations, December 2010 Recruit pages, and the August 2011 beta exit?
