## Bounded objective and scope

Reconstruct the first employer paying accounts and early monetization of InterviewStreet/HackerRank from the first candidate-side paid activity (reported around 2009) through the early employer-product and brand-consolidation period (through 2016). Identify what was sold, to whom, by what route, and what each source actually establishes about demos, use, client status, contract, payment, and revenue.

The main focus is early employer accounts and commercial mechanics. Candidate-side practice/interview revenue is included only to separate it from employer revenue and clarify reported amounts. Do not build a full customer roster, valuation model, exhaustive competitor history, or later-stage enterprise sales history. Pursue 10/100-customer milestones only if directly supported by evidence encountered within scope.

## Investigation strategy (planned, not executed)

1. Start with the named milestone conflicts in `GAP-0001`: Flipkart client/use, BankBazaar first-check, WisdomTap referral, and the unnamed five word-of-mouth payers.
2. Search freely accessible buyer/company announcements, archived InterviewStreet/HackerRank product and pricing pages, customer-side hiring/engineering records, public accelerator/company histories, and contemporaneous independent reporting.
3. Use company customer stories and founder interviews to generate or qualify leads; seek independent or buyer-side corroboration for account status and payment. Preserve original source lineage and date semantics.
4. Reconcile pricing pages with reported actual receipts only when the source identifies product, payer, currency, period, and transaction basis.
5. Search for named-account acquisition routes and contemporaneous lead attribution. A candidate application/interview funnel or an employer logo does not establish how the employer was acquired or whether it paid.

**Competing explanations:** Founder/friend networks and referrals acquired early accounts; word of mouth compounded first use; employer urgent hiring needs drove direct product adoption; job-site recruiter inbound generated leads; community/contests sourced candidates and later created employer demand; more than one route operated at different stages.

**Evidence constraints:** Public/free sources only; no paid access, external interviews, outreach, or contacting customers. Retrospective claims remain attributed. An absent public transaction record is not proof no payment occurred.

## Independent review and learning
