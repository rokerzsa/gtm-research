# End-to-end completion plan v1

**Status:** Proposed, awaiting explicit combined approval. Current P2 execution remains authorized under DEC-0005; this proposal does not extend its 25-source ceiling.

## Outcome and scope

Complete the HackerRank historical GTM book from InterviewStreet origins through the present research cutoff, 11 November 2026. Follow charter v1 subject to the explicit completion/dependency amendment below. Free public sources only; no outreach, paid access, valuation model or exhaustive competitor history. No personalized lessons about the user's business. All work remains on work/next-updates; publication/merging is a separate action unless requested.

## Remaining sequence and chapters

1. Finish the bounded P2 first-payer/account/pricing investigation using its existing balance. Review chapters 01 and 02, selectively amend them and record a criterion-by-criterion disposition.
2. P3: community and employer growth, chiefly 2011–2018. Chapter 03 explains contests, developer participation and employer discovery; chapter 04 explains founder sales, recurring software sales, buyer segments and the sales organization. Test referrals/direct sales versus community-caused acquisition and separate candidate hiring outcomes from employer conversion.
3. P4: material shifts 2018–2026. Chapter 05 explains scale, global/upmarket development and remote-hiring-era workflow changes; chapter 06 explains the later AI-era product, assessment and positioning changes. Anchor chapters in dated product workflows, buyer needs, acquisition, monetization and outcomes, without forcing an event into an assumed causal story.
4. P5: chapter 07 draws together the historical arc, failed/changed approaches and a few developed transferable lessons with limits. Compare rival growth explanations; perform cross-chapter chronology, source and editorial reviews. Review and finalize each chapter before moving to the closing chapter, then perform a whole-book review.

Chapter boundaries may be refined within these questions for narrative coherence, with recorded reasons; no additional scope is implied.

## Research and review method

Question-driven public primary/contemporary evidence, with independent reporting and attributed retrospectives. Record actual source access, lineage, concrete claims and counterevidence. Search for disconfirmation after freezing each major conclusion; same investigator in a separate source-seeking pass is permitted and honestly disclosed. No invented independent reviewer. Editorial review reads each story without notes; evidence review traces substantive claims and preserves credible alternatives.

## Completion and dependency amendment for approval

Allow P3–P5 to proceed from the reviewed P1/P2 evidence handoff without claiming the unresolved first-payer or community-caused-sales facts have been verified. Permit final completion with clearly documented historical unknowns after substantive recovery/disconfirmation attempts, an explicit impact/disposition record and accurate treatment in the book. Unperformed mandatory investigation/review, unsupported confident assertions, unrecorded material contradictions, broken outputs or unrecovered state still block completion. This changes the charter v1 rule that any unresolved critical historical gap prevents project closure; it does not change historical confidence or permit hiding a gap.

## Resources

For P3–P5, propose no numeric source/time cap, consistent with the user's original no-cap preference; measure distinct inspected source lineages and avoid repetitive low-information sources. No paid access or outreach. No source-count quota or arbitrary requirement to exhaust the web. Continue while material questions have promising accessible avenues; record bounded reasons when further searching has low expected information gain.

Stable source/claim/review records, meaningful journal and gap updates, chapter-level editorial/evidence review log, book versions preserved outside published selection, navigable reader-only Docusaurus book, phase dossiers and final charter audit. Build and local-link checks must pass. Final book states research cutoff and consequential unknowns. No claim of universal lessons or causal attribution beyond evidence.
