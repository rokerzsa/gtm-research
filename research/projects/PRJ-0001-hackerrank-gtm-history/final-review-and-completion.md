## Whole-book editorial finding

The reader can follow candidate practice, employer screening, developer participation, recurring employer workflows, remote hiring and changing AI assessment needs. Each stage identifies what someone actually does before explaining a strategy. The closing chapter derives conditional lessons rather than claiming one proven engine. No fabricated scene, single neat pivot, audited customer curve, independent AI validity or universal business recommendation is used to fill gaps. The evidence supports a connected history more strongly than a quantified causal growth model.
