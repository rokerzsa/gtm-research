## 1. Objective and scope

**Objective:** Establish what the early InterviewStreet/HackerRank company sold, who first paid, what product and price/revenue model applied, and how early employer opportunities were acquired, while preserving distinctions among candidate payments, demos, use, clients, contracts, payers, and revenue.

**In scope:** Early candidate-side paid activity and employer commercialization from the reported 2009 origins through the 2016 retrospective evidence horizon; first employer accounts and early account acquisition; pricing, transaction, and payment claims; focused review of community, referrals, founder network, word of mouth, direct sales, and recruiter inbound where tied to early accounts.

**Excluded:** Paid access; external outreach/interviews; valuation or investor returns; exhaustive customer roster; exhaustive competitor history; later-stage GTM beyond the early-account question except for a directly relevant corroborating record.

**Key definitions:** Distinguish user/participant, candidate applicant/interviewee, employer demo/trial, product user, client/customer, signed account/contract, payer/first payer, and recognized or reported revenue. Treat list price as a price offer, not a completed transaction.

## 4. Investigation strategy (execute only if authorized)

**Research avenues:** Public/free archived pricing and product pages; customer-side employer hiring or engineering pages; public company/accelerator statements; dated customer stories; contemporaneous reporting; public institutional records and event materials; founder retrospectives as attributed leads. Use source-specific avenues in P2.1 plan.

**Order/depth rationale:** Resolve named early account and payment conflicts first; then identify product/price/transaction distinctions; then test account-level acquisition channels and reported early counts.

**Alternative explanations and falsification:** Direct or founder-network sales, referrals, word of mouth, recruiter inbound, employer-led urgent need, contest/community-sourced talent, and mixed routes. A customer transaction without a lead-source record does not establish channel causality. A candidate hiring outcome does not establish employer acquisition.

**Legitimate missing-source recovery paths:** Freely accessible web archives, public buyer/company archives, public press/accelerator archives, and public institutional records only. No paid access or outreach.

## 5. Evidence and source expectations

Seek dated buyer-side or transaction records for first payer, product, price, and payment. Contemporaneous first-party product/pricing pages can support what was offered, not that it sold. Independent reporting and retrospective founder/customer stories can support attributed claims while retaining limitations. Preserve dates, original source lineage, access status, and failed recovery attempts. Do not infer a payment from a logo, customer story, or “client” label.
