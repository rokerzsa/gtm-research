## Mandatory questions and adequate outcomes

- P3-Q1: What did programmers do in the community/contests, and how did employers encounter candidates? Establish actual workflows and dated changes, not just participation totals.
- P3-Q2: Which routes brought employers to trial, purchase or expansion? Test referrals, founder/direct/enterprise selling, partnerships, events and community against account-level evidence where accessible. Unknown channel shares remain qualified.
- P3-Q3: How did the buyer, sales organization and charging model develop through 2018? Distinguish plans, reported receipts, customer labels and documented commercial practices.
- P4-Q1: What material changes in segments, geography, product workflow and distribution occurred from 2018 to 2026, and what pressures did actors state caused them? Verify current claims as of the research cutoff.
- P4-Q2: How did remote hiring and AI affect what employers bought and what the company sold? Distinguish announcements, availability, claimed performance and observed outcomes; test market-timing and competitive alternatives.
- P5-Q1: Which explanations survive adversarial source investigation, and what remains unknown? Every major aggregate/causal conclusion must have durable RT coverage and an honest verdict.
- P5-Q2: What concrete lessons follow from the company's turning points, under which conditions, and with what limits? No application to the user's current business.
