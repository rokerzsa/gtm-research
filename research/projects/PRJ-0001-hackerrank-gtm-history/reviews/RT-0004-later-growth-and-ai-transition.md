These are observed announced changes, not proof of uninterrupted success or of any feature's incremental revenue effect. ## Counterhypotheses

Remote growth could reflect temporary market demand rather than durable product advantage; expansion plans could fail financially; AI positioning may be marketing ahead of adoption; proctoring/detection and AI-fluency claims may be unreliable; post-hire products could diversify sales but their actual contribution is unknown. Seek buyer-side workflow/outcomes, original technical documentation, contemporaneous independent criticism and market constraints. Original IANS email not inspected, and an inconsistent launch-year sentence must not contaminate chronology. Freeze before the next independent search pass.

## Strongest objections and adjudication

- A smooth-success narrative fails: IANS's reported CEO email describes financial difficulty and customer-protection priorities after the growth/funding period. Attribute the report; the original email is not available. Do not reuse its erroneous launch-year line.
- Remote product causality is not isolated: ServiceNow describes implementation/training as part of adoption, while the bookings release coincides with remote labor demand. Product, customer-success work and market timing can all matter. No controlled attribution exists.
- AI product performance remains unverified: technical documentation makes the workflow more concrete, but neither that nor a release page validates scoring/predictive accuracy. The 2022 research abstract supplies a real task/memorization concern, not a test of 2026 detection.
- Stated controls and AI-enabled work are different policies: 2023 detection and 2026 AI-allowed evaluation are not necessarily a reversal if different tasks have different instructions. The later blog criticizes blanket prohibition, but does not establish every earlier assessment was abandoned.
- Company retrospective counts/dates are inconsistent: current about-page blocks mix periods and its funding history conflicts with contemporaneous releases. Reject one common customer-growth chart and exact funding chronology derived from this page. EMEA opening is retained only as a company retrospective; regional revenue impact unknown.
