## Frozen working proposition

The public evidence distinguishes an early noncash/referral relationship, employer product use, and a founder-recalled cash payment. It does not verify the first paying employer. Early monetization involved several charging units/products; the inspected prices and client counts do not support a single comparable price or paid-account growth series. Formal annual seat selling is described contemporaneously, while the magnitude and source of resulting paid growth remain unverified.

**Targets:** CLM-0051, CLM-0071–0072, CLM-0076–0077, CLM-0079–0095; GAP-0001.  
**Original support:** Existing first-client/check/referral accounts; SRC-0026–0036, including conflicting account definitions, separate plan descriptions, a commercial form and the sales-director interview.  
**Major trigger:** Misidentifying first payer, equating clients with paid accounts, or treating the apparent price/model sequence as a proven cause of growth would materially change the case study.  
**Initial evidence/confidence:** High that the sources make the attributed statements; low for verified first payment and account-level causal contribution. No aggregate review passed.

## Adversarial questions and rival explanations

1. Can a public customer-side record establish payment/order and date for BankBazaar, Flipkart or WisdomTap?
2. Does dated contemporary evidence force a different first-payer sequence from later recollections?
3. Are the customer totals explicitly defined in a way that makes the apparent discrepancies comparable?
4. Do primary plan artifacts show a single successive pricing ladder, or does contemporaneous selling distinguish product, seat, usage, billing period and negotiated orders?
5. Can buyer-side adoption evidence attribute acquisition to the developer community rather than founder/direct/referral routes?

**Planned avenues:** Named customer/partner domains, contemporaneous official posts, public dated pricing artifacts and retrospective source-lineage checks. No paid access or outreach; no claim of exhaustive public-web coverage.

## Actual adversarial investigation

After freezing the proposition, searched named buyer/partner domains and combinations of BankBazaar, Flipkart, WisdomTap, Indeed and employer-payment terms. Results returned existing founder recollections, candidate experiences and derivative profiles; none inspected established a dated first payment or community-originated paid account. Reopened SRC-0015/0024/0025 without duplicate budget draw. Derivative retellings were not counted as independent corroboration.

Attempted Wayback recovery of InterviewStreet Recruit pricing near August 2011 and HackerRankX pricing near February 2014; both requests were inaccessible. No snapshot content or price was inferred from the requested URL. Search surfaced the dated November 2013 CodePair thread, actually inspected as SRC-0037 / CLM-0096 and charged once. This counterfinding requires separating a public demonstration from the February 2014 announcement/commercial coverage.
