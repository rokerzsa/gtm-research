## Continuation checkpoint update — after SRC-0021–0025

### What changed in the evidence

1. **A candidate funnel is now visible at intermediate steps.** TechCrunch reported CodeSprint participants, applications, and final interviews. HackerRank's drchrono story reports applicant screening and challenge-related hiring. This supports candidate sourcing and movement toward interviews for employers using the service. It does not show the route by which those employers became customers or paying accounts.
2. **The early employer route is still described through introductions and word of mouth.** BusinessToday retrospectively calls Flipkart the first corporate client and says five more paying clients came through word of mouth. Fortune India describes a WisdomTap referral leading to a Flipkart demo and later use; it names BankBazaar and Amazon among paying customers without identifying the first payer. These claims differ in detail and have no invoice or buyer-side confirmation.
3. **The monetary milestones refer to different products and statuses.** Fortune India reports approximately ₹4,000 from mock interviews after nearly 1.5 years, one student's ₹1,000 practice-test payment, free WisdomTap use for a referral, a Flipkart demo/use sequence, and other employer payers. They do not reconcile the separate “25,000” mock-interview figure or Vivek's 20–30 interview estimate.
4. **The community rationale is clearer than its commercial effect.** Hari attributed the idea to YC advice that talent sourcing mattered more to employers than assessment. BusinessToday describes monthly contests and hiring winners. These accounts support stated intent and a candidate-hiring mechanism, not community-caused employer revenue. “Born in 2011,” 2012 community activity, and 2012 public launch may describe different stages; exact dates remain open.

### Deep reflection questions

**LRN-0004 — Where does a talent funnel stop being evidence of sales?**

> Map the reported chain from participant → applicant → interview → hire. Separately map employer discovery → lead → demo/trial → contract → payment. Which links are evidenced in CodeSprint and the drchrono story, and which remain assumptions? What data would join the two chains without confusing the employer's use of a product with the channel that acquired the employer?

**LRN-0005 — What does “first customer” mean here?**

> Compare BusinessToday's Flipkart “first corporate client,” Fortune India's Flipkart demo and later use, the early company case study, and Vivek's BankBazaar first-check account. Which statements can coexist if client, user, and payer are distinct? What single record would most directly establish the first paid employer account?

**LRN-0006 — How should we weigh a plausible mechanism against rival channels?**

> The founder says sourcing talent motivated the community; the sources also report friend introductions, a WisdomTap referral, and word-of-mouth customer acquisition. What causal claim is justified at each strength level: product intent, candidate sourcing, employer hiring use, employer lead generation, or paid sales? What comparison or counterfactual would distinguish community-driven revenue from direct and referral-led sales?

**Current judgment:** The evidence supports deliberate product linkage and candidate-sourcing/screening mechanisms. It does not establish that community activity generated a paid employer account. Keep `RT-0001` unresolved, `GAP-0001` critical/open, and P1.1 blocked/incomplete. The proposed P2.1 plan is a separate governance decision.
