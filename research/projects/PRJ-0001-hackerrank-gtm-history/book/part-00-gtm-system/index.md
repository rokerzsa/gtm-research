# Understanding the company GTM system

Before tracing individual decisions, it helps to see how the business fits together. The [complete GTM chapter](00-full-gtm-system.md) connects customer problems, routes into an employer account, evaluation, purchase, delivery and continued use. It also shows where current offers extend that system and where performance remains unknown.

This is the map for the deeper chapters, rather than a substitute for their explanations. Start with the [book introduction](../index.md) for the purpose and reading path, then use the complete system to orient the choices examined afterward.
