## Intended outcome

Produce research that lets a strategy reader reconstruct consequential choices and explain how the commercial system developed. More sources, longer chapters, additional checklists and a complete portfolio inventory are not sufficient outcomes.

For material decisions, the book should connect the prior situation, trigger, choice, commitment, implementation, expected commercial mechanism, subsequent evidence and next move. Attribute management explanations; distinguish analyst interpretation, rival explanations and observed results. Missing private evidence must limit claims without erasing useful public history or reasonable bounded interpretation.

Acquisitions are the immediate diagnostic example. The same standard must work for pricing, product changes, channel choices, partnerships, geographic expansion and organizational changes. It must not turn every launch into a pivot or force every company into an acquisition chapter.

## Implementation sequence and completion criteria

### 1. Establish the evaluation cases before changing instructions

Use the inspected passages as bounded evaluation inputs. Specify the central reader question and expected disposition before judging a revision. Include raw source passages, not only favorable record summaries. Store results in one plan-linked evaluation report; do not introduce another evidence namespace or require an ID for every field.

Completion: each case below has an expected outcome and a reason grounded in the source or reader question. These are planned tests, not tests already passed.

### 2. Amend the skill coherently

Implement P0 first, then the small P1 changes. Maintain progressive disclosure and remove redundant generic wording where the new test supersedes it. Reuse the existing four reviews and research records. Preserve public/free access limits, source honesty, actual hard ceilings, autonomous in-scope corrections, separate evidence/review states, no routine extra approvals, and checkpoint-last behavior.

Completion: entrypoint, operational references, specification and templates agree; links resolve; no fixed chapter/source counts, mandatory deal tables for every company, invented probabilities or requirement to recover private financial data appears.

### 3. Pilot on UpGrad's acquisition and strategy sequence

Work in the existing project and preserve the prior manuscript. Inventory material completed, announced, failed and consolidated transactions using current evidence first. Reconcile dates/amounts/definitions; identify true research gaps separately from facts already available. Investigate only the gaps that materially affect the explanation, within existing scope and cutoff.

Test a working interpretation rather than assuming it: did the portfolio evolve through capability/access expansion followed by attempts to coordinate the group, or do target-specific opportunities and changing conditions better explain parts of the sequence? Compare contemporaneous statements and observable actions. Neither reading is established by this plan.

Use a compact chronology where helpful, then explain the consequential episodes and connections. Do not merely add prices to the existing table. Connect INSOFE's stated capability/offline ambition with later execution evidence while preserving dispute boundaries. Treat Unacademy as a consolidation episode with its own target context, status and commitment; do not infer post-close success. Revise the canonical overview and lessons only after the analysis supports them.

Completion: a reader can reconstruct the material sequence, distinguish commitments from forecasts, explain case-specific mechanisms and limits, and understand how later actions relate to earlier bets. Missing available facts are repaired; unavoidable unknowns do not block the whole book by default.

### 4. Review the complete book, then apply the method to the other cases

After the pilot passes, review UpGrad beyond acquisitions: pricing/premium support, marketing/distribution, institutions/geography, economics and AI. Do not let fixing the reported example substitute for whole-book usefulness.

Review HackerRank around its actual commercial transitions and RedyHire around its documented customer/operating constraints. Preserve strong concrete episodes. Expand investigation only where a central question cannot be answered; accept thinner historical detail when honestly investigated. Do not force the same chapter structure or information density across firms.

Completion: each book's review names specific answers, weak passages repaired and remaining evidence limits. Update existing review/revision/journal records and checkpoints last after actual changes. Keep earlier favorable reviews as superseded history where necessary; do not silently mark them current.

## Planned behavioral evaluation

| Input or failure case | Required result |
|---|---|
| Accurate, heavily cited acquisition capability inventory with generic benefits and caveats | Fail strategic depth; identify the unanswered reader question |
| INSOFE extraction omits the disclosed consideration | Fail material-fact reconciliation; repair the record before relying on derived analysis |
| Unacademy record contains consideration but book omits it without an editorial reason | Fail synthesis reconciliation for a transaction-history question |
| M&A allocation presented as KnowledgeHut purchase price, or a forecast as achieved revenue | Fail evidence interpretation |
| Rich chronology and numbers with no explanation of strategic significance | Fail depth; facts alone are insufficient |
| “They bought it to reduce CAC” with no attributable rationale or supporting mechanism | Fail unsupported intent/causality; investigate, qualify or remove |
| Supported interpretation with stated basis, credible alternatives and discriminating evidence | May pass without access to internal deliberations |
| Undisclosed price after reasonable investigation, with useful strategy explanation | May pass qualified; no fabricated estimate or endless search |
| Founder payment recollection turned into verified first-customer price | Fail calibration; retain attribution and uncertainty |
| RedyHire operating decision explained concretely, commercial impact unknown | May pass that episode; does not automatically pass whole-company strategy |
| Every product launch divided into a new historical pivot | Fail periodization; preserve overlapping offers and distinguish material changes |
| Company name can be swapped while the purported finding remains equally applicable | Rework the finding with case evidence or label it as general context |

Run these as disclosed same-investigator evaluations with expected versus observed results. A static validator cannot certify strategic quality; do not claim independent reader testing. An additional reviewer is optional only if separately available and authorized, not a new dependency.

## Remaining uncertainty

This sample establishes real extraction, synthesis and review failures. It does not establish how frequently they occur across every chapter or source. The pilot must also test whether extra detail improves the argument rather than creating a larger evidence dump. The user's dissatisfaction is evidence that prior delivery missed the intended usefulness; it is not evidence for a particular corporate strategy or an automatic factual-confidence downgrade.
