## Substantive adversarial review

Freeze exact major targets, confidence and original evidence before a distinct source-seeking challenge. Seek disconfirmation, missing current offers, competing mechanisms and alternatives. A separate model is optional; disclose same-investigator separate passes honestly. Preserve evidence state separately from review state; qualify or retain unresolved disputes without inventing certainty. In-scope review and corrective research are autonomous under the consolidated approval. Strategy depth and full GTM coverage are review targets, not just citation correctness.
