# Increment 1 — Scope, limits, and review guide

[Book index](../index.md) · [SKILL.md](../../SKILL.md)

## Delivered in this increment

- Discoverable Codex `SKILL.md` with frontmatter and narrow GTM research activation.
- Standalone architecture: **no Goal Memory dependency**.
- Required human authorization boundaries and current foundation-only stop condition.
- Workspace root, document responsibility and stable identity conventions.
- Approved 14-chapter/5-part navigation map and a primary DR-001–DR-052 coverage index.
- Audit reconciliations and the resulting standalone amendment.
- Non-executable workspace record templates illustrating minimal Markdown conventions.
- Static review checklist.

## Explicitly not delivered

- Installation into a user's local Codex installation or repository.
- Creation of a live `gtm-research/` workspace or company research project.
- Complete materialization of all fourteen detailed specification chapters.
- Operational evidence investigation, provenance and claim registries, independent red-team, adaptive budgets, synthesis generation, learning workflows, completion/reopening engines, or automated tests.
- Any scripts, external service access, paid research or runtime validation.

## Review sequence

1. Inspect [SKILL.md](../../SKILL.md) for appropriate activation and hard stops.
2. Inspect [standalone OKF conventions](../../references/standalone-okf-conventions.md) for separation from Goal Memory, counters and safe write order.
3. Check [decision traceability](decision-coverage.md) and [audit reconciliations](audit-reconciliations.md).
4. Verify book navigation and the lack of broken links.
5. Use [static checklist](../../references/static-review-checklist.md) and approve or request changes.
