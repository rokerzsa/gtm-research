## Safeguards retained

- Research remains **standalone** and does not depend on Goal Memory.
- Charter, high-level phase and immediately detailed plan/contract must be approved before investigative or red-team activity outside bounded reconnaissance.
- Source provenance, independent information lineage, claim support/counterevidence and separate confidence/evidence/review states are preserved.
- Major causal/strategic findings remain review `pending` until an *actually performed* independent red-team pass reaches a justified disposition.
- Authorization to **consume** preauthorized extension is not permission to **expand** the ceiling; unapproved paid access and unverifiable hard-limit overrun are prohibited.
- Critical gaps and unresolved material disputes retain escalation and dependency protections; neither a review nor a budget event constitutes research completion.
- Stable workspace `K` and project-scoped `SRC`/`CLM`/`RT`/`DEC`/`GAP` identities, ordered writes, single-writer recovery and checkpoint-last remain applicable.
