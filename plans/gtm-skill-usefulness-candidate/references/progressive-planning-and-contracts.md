# Full-run planning with autonomous refinement

[Authorization](activation-and-approval-gates.md) · [Plan](../templates/research-plan.md) · [Contract](../templates/research-contract.md)

The charter defines the assignment; the plan organizes work; contracts specify executable questions and reviews. These are internal views of **one approved execution agreement**, not separate human approval objects.

## Prepare the full run

Before approval describe objectives, boundaries, strategy/current-state questions, discovery avenues, important rival explanations, resource constraints, review criteria, treatment of unknowns and review-ready deliverables. Include workspace/root and explicit goal-start authorization. Plan research stages around dependencies and information gain, with enough tactical discretion to follow newly discovered products, pivots and buyer types.

Do not freeze a final table of contents before understanding the company. Require one complete whole-company GTM chapter; choose deeper chapters from findings. Planning stages and chapter boundaries can differ.

## After approval

Record the actual decision once. Derive/refine internal stage contracts and coverage maps under that authority; link each to the agreement/version and actual decision. Do not invent approval of a later document version: state that its questions are covered by the existing agreement. Reprioritize, cross-check present products alongside historical work, revisit earlier claims and change the outline when the findings warrant it. Preserve consequential decisions, not an approval ceremony for every adjustment.

Use a goal only when explicitly requested/authorized and available; its objective is the full agreed review-ready deliverable. Keep evidence/checkpoint in OKF. If goals are unavailable, continue authorized execution through the checkpoint and disclose the capability limit. Do not call repeated per-phase approvals a substitute for autonomous execution.

## Change boundary

A finding that changes the answer to an agreed question is not itself a scope change. Pause only affected action for a genuinely different objective/subject, essential missing user decision, hard-limit increase, paid access/outreach or external action without authority. Reopening and internal review do not grant new spending or override a ceiling. Preserve existing project decisions and limits on resume.
