# Activation and the consolidated execution agreement

[Book home](../book/index.md) · [Intake](goal-discovery-and-charter.md) · [Planning](progressive-planning-and-contracts.md)

Use full research when evidence complexity, causal importance and uncertainty warrant it. Simple factual requests need ordinary lookup; a borderline task may need one question about intended depth.

## One informed approval

Bounded reconnaissance and intake prepare one reviewable agreement: objective, subjects/timeframe, audience, strategic questions, workspace location, constraints, allowed methods, whole-company GTM chapter/adaptive deep dives, review criteria, acceptable unknowns, final user-review boundary and explicit permission to start a goal. One actual approval covers the charter, full research plan and internal contracts. Internal stages are not fresh permission gates. A root named and approved in this agreement needs no second initialization approval; initialize non-destructively under existing conventions.

| Action | Treatment |
|---|---|
| In-scope source discovery, hypothesis revision, current-product follow-up, phase progression and internal review | Proceed autonomously under the approved agreement |
| Add/reorder/merge chapters, refine strategic emphasis, correct evidence | Proceed; preserve meaningful changes and evidence mappings |
| Investigated unknown with an agreed qualified disposition | Document attempts, impact, limits and revisit trigger; do not invent certainty or seek ceremonial approval |
| Essential missing preference or genuinely changed objective/subject | Ask the specific decision needed; continue unaffected work |
| Exceed a hard ceiling, paid source, outreach or external action without authority | Stop that action and request explicit authorization |
| Final internally reviewed book | Deliver ready for user review, then stop autonomous research; acceptance is not inferred |

Decisions must be real, versioned and durable; a contract's existence does not authorize it. Existing projects retain prior permissions and hard limits; reconcile rather than silently rewrite history. New findings that alter the explanation within the agreed question are normal research, not scope expansion. Publication, installation and messaging require their own applicable authorization.

Evidence confidence, RT review state, execution readiness and user acceptance are separate. See [completion](completion-and-acceptance.md), [budgets](research-budget-management.md) and [recovery](recovery-and-consistency.md).
