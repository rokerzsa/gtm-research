# Increment 2 — Static Review Checklist

[Book home](../book/index.md) · [Increment 2 review record](../book/appendices/increment-2-review.md)

**Scope:** specification and static file inspection, **not a live execution test**. Tick only after verifying actual file content. All checks must be reported as pass/fail/qualified in the review output.

- [ ] Codex `SKILL.md` frontmatter is valid, with narrow GTM triggering and an explicit Increment 2-only capability boundary.
- [ ] No Goal Memory / `memory temp/` dependency or shadow authoritative store is introduced.
- [ ] Lightweight, borderline and full deep-research modes differ by complexity/evidence needs rather than response length.
- [ ] Reconnaissance is explicitly bounded and does not silently become substantive source investigation.
- [ ] All **13** mandatory charter discovery dimensions are covered in procedure and template.
- [ ] Adaptive goal-grilling challenges consequential assumptions, ambiguity, alternatives and accepted unknowns.
- [ ] The charter distinguishes approved requirements from open questions, hypotheses and unapproved extensions.
- [ ] Charter approval, high-level plan approval and immediate detailed-plan approval are separate gates.
- [ ] Combined detailed-plan/attached-contract approval is unambiguous and does not collapse other gates.
- [ ] Research contracts contain identity, scope, questions, dependencies, method, evidence, red-team triggers, budgets, outputs, completion conditions and state.
- [ ] Contracts cannot extend an approved plan without consequential amendment approval.
- [ ] Version/amendment history and real human decision evidence are required; no fictional `approved` state.
- [ ] Workspace initialization and persistent writes remain authorized, recoverable and checkpoint-last.
- [ ] Templates are clearly draft/non-executable and do not imply actual research outputs or source access.
- [ ] Full Chapters 01–02 are present, link from the book index and Part I index, and reflect the standalone revision.
- [ ] Other unmaterialized chapters remain explicitly pending; Chapter 03 implementation claims **contract preparation only**.
- [ ] All Markdown internal links to included files resolve; no broken navigation or fictional links.
- [ ] DR-001–DR-052 primary coverage remains complete; R-01–R-08 and identity namespaces are preserved.
- [ ] Package does not claim research execution, actual red-team reviews, budget consumption, learning execution, completion or full release readiness.
- [ ] No live workspace, installed skill, paid service, substantive company research or Increment 3 work was created.

**Static review disclaimer:** Correct file structure and instruction text do not demonstrate reliable execution in a real Codex runtime; later increments and human review gates remain necessary.
