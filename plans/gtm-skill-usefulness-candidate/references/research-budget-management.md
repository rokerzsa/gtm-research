# Proportionate research effort and real hard limits

[Authorization](activation-and-approval-gates.md) · [Ledger](../templates/research-budget-ledger.md)

Record only meaningful configured dimensions: actual source investigations/passes and user-assigned time, token, money or other limits. Unassigned limits stay unassigned. Do not propose arbitrary per-phase source caps or extension ceremonies merely to make a ledger look complete. No source-count quota demonstrates depth or evidence exhaustion.

Track actual consumption as measured, estimated with basis, or unavailable. Never invent token/cost/time counters or report zero for unknown use. Distinct source lineages are counted once, with inspected excerpts labeled honestly. Avoid double-counting the same consumption across hierarchy; internal allocations do not raise a parent hard ceiling.

Before a resource-consuming action check all actual applicable ceilings, access commitments and remaining enforceable capacity. Existing approved caps remain binding; new workflow instructions do not remove them. If measurement/upper bound cannot enforce a real hard limit, use a safely bounded option or ask the specific needed decision. No absence of a money cap authorizes paid access.

Proceed within existing authority. An explicitly preauthorized extension may be consumed with a brief information-gain rationale inside every ceiling; never fabricate or enlarge one. Exceeding a hard ceiling, paid access or outreach requires explicit authority. Do not relabel work into another phase to bypass a cap; continue unaffected questions while a decision is pending.

Reassess by coverage, important uncertainty, distinct available avenues and expected information gain. Stop repetitive low-value searching; do not consume allowance simply because it remains. A hard limit may force incomplete delivery, but cannot validate a conclusion. An investigated unknown can be qualified only under the [agreed completion criteria](completion-and-acceptance.md).

Persist meaningful events and actual authorizations before checkpoint; reconcile interrupted state before further usage. An internal ledger or goal is not spending permission. Skill installation/packaging/publication requires applicable action authorization, not a research-effort entry.
