# Adaptive human learning checkpoints

[Book home](../book/index.md) · [Chapter 12](../book/part-04-outputs-lifecycle/12-human-learning.md) · [Checkpoint template](../templates/learning-checkpoint.md) · [Approval gates](activation-and-approval-gates.md)

**Principle:** Teach through the investigation's most meaningful findings and challenge interpretations, but separate **learning comprehension**, **evidence status**, and **human authorization**. Learning discussions are configurable and nonblocking for unrelated research unless the charter or an existing consequential gate explicitly requires a response.

## 1. Charter configuration

At goal discovery, capture as appropriate: learner's prior knowledge, desired depth, checkpoint intensity (`light`, `balanced`, `deep`, or custom), preferred kinds of exercises, relevant GTM concepts to master, and whether any particular human review is required. Do not claim these preferences without the user's input. A later preference adjustment must not silently amend substantive research objectives.

A *light* mode might explain findings on demand; a *deep* mode may examine rival models and ask the human to propose discriminating evidence. Neither setting changes evidence quality.

## 2. Meaningful checkpoint triggers

Consider offering a checkpoint when:

- A subphase reveals a consequential or surprising discovery.
- A major causal conclusion and its red-team challenge can be compared.
- An important early-customer or channel assumption changes.
- A phase boundary exposes alternative GTM trajectories.
- A case-specific lesson raises a useful question about the researched company's choices or the general conditions behind its outcome.

Avoid quizzes after every source or trivial paragraph. When a checkpoint would disrupt independent work, defer the discussion and continue only unaffected authorized investigation. A checkpoint cannot silently override a true charter approval requirement.

## 3. Session structure

1. State the question and what the evidence *actually* shows, using stable `K`/`CLM`/`SRC` and relevant `RT` references.
2. Briefly expose uncertainty, direct vs reported evidence and one or two meaningful competing interpretations.
3. Ask a **targeted reasoning question**, such as which alternative explanation the learner favors and what contemporaneous evidence would distinguish it.
4. Explore the human's answer respectfully; explain strengths, missed assumptions and counterevidence. Do not use superficial recall or ceremonial agreement.
5. Explain the lesson through the researched company and its business conditions. Do not import the learner's company, product, industry or strategy from conversation history. Connect a finding to that business only on a separate explicit request.
6. Summarize what was learned and optional follow-up questions without modifying source/claim evidence states based solely on learner agreement.

## 4. Learning note and nonblocking state

A brief [learning-checkpoint template](../templates/learning-checkpoint.md) can store an event/anchor, project and finding references, discussion questions, optional learner interpretation, misconceptions or open questions, `optional`/`requested`/`completed` status, and next reading links. Do not fabricate a learner answer if none was given. Use local log anchors where useful, not a new global ID allocator. Record sensitive personal notes only where justified and authorized.

**Nonblocking default:** A pending teaching discussion does not stop independent work. Mark `blocking` only if the approved charter or separately required human governance decision makes a response essential. An explanation request never authorizes new paid access, extra budget, a revised charter or a different research interpretation.

## 5. GTM-specific example (not company evidence)

> "We found an archived acquisition campaign and a later account attributing early growth to it. Why does the first source establish the campaign's existence but not its causal lift? What would you seek to tell campaign-led growth apart from founder-network-led sales?"

The learner's response informs the next teaching exchange, not the status of a historical fact. A major GTM interpretation must still undergo its applicable independent red-team review regardless of the learner's confidence.

## 6. Learning versus project lifecycle boundary

These learning procedures may accompany a draft or completed research book, but **learning comprehension is never a completion criterion unless explicitly required by the approved charter**. Actual [subphase/project completion](completion-and-acceptance.md) and [selective reopening](dependency-impact-and-reopening.md) have independent evidence and authorization gates. Skill installation/release remains separately human-gated.
