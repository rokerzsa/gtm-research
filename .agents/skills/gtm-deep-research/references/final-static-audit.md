# Skill consistency and behavioral validation

[Specification](../book/index.md) · [Current amendment](../book/appendices/audit-reconciliations.md)

## Static checks

Validate frontmatter/name/description and all changed references. Inspect live entrypoint, procedures, templates and specification for contradictory separate routine approvals, frozen chapter structures, blanket unknown blockers, goal-memory dependence and false acceptance states. Classify historical increment/review artifacts rather than rewriting them as new evidence. Retain namespaces, source-access honesty, evidence/review separation, actual hard ceilings and recovery/checkpoint-last safeguards.

## Behavioral checks

Use bounded realistic requests and raw artifacts to evaluate: context-aware upfront intake; one informed approval; complete company GTM overview with adaptive deep dives; discovering and analyzing material current brands/buyers; changes in interpretation/outline within scope; investigated unknown versus unperformed work; actual hard-cap/paid/outreach boundaries; planned versus shipped future direction; stop at review-ready delivery with acceptance pending. Check strategic choices, alternatives, trade-offs and GTM implications, not merely named product presence. For writing revisions, inspect raw introduction, overview, pivot and conclusion text in reading order: context and purpose must precede judgment, chapters must advance a shared inquiry, and section transitions must not invent causality. Table-of-contents and link validity do not establish coherence.

A historical-only scope must respect its cutoff. No fixed chapter/source quotas, company-specific generic rules, invented independent reviewer or automatic project migration. An independent model pass can add confidence when available and authorized; give it the skill/request/raw artifacts rather than a desired answer. Use isolated outputs and do not contact outsiders or mutate live research projects during evaluation.

## Usefulness evaluation cases

For changes to investigation, synthesis or review, select bounded raw evidence and draft excerpts that exercise the affected behavior. State the reader question and expected disposition before evaluation. Compare observed judgments/repairs with these expectations and record the actual outputs and limits. Use both weak and useful cases; do not validate against favorable summaries alone. These are evaluation examples, not quotas for every study or proof of autonomous end-to-end reliability.

| Input | Expected judgment or repair |
|---|---|
| Cited capability inventory plus generic benefits and caveats; reader asks how strategy developed | Fail depth; identify and reconstruct the missing decision explanation |
| Inspected transaction table discloses consideration but extraction omits it | Fail material-fact reconciliation; repair records before derived synthesis |
| Record preserves a material deal commitment but transaction-history chapter drops it | Repair synthesis unless an explicit editorial reason preserves the reader's answer elsewhere |
| M&A allocation labeled one deal's price; forecast labeled achieved sales | Fail definition/time fidelity; separate the amounts and states |
| Detailed chronology and amounts without strategic significance | Fail depth; explain the choice/mechanism rather than adding more facts |
| Purchase attributed to lowering customer acquisition cost with no evidentiary basis | Reject invented intent/causality; investigate, qualify a supported hypothesis or remove |
| Conditional rationale with evidence, credible alternatives and discriminating signals | May pass without internal deliberation documents; do not upgrade to proven motive/results |
| Price undisclosed after reasonable investigation; decision otherwise usefully explained | May pass qualified; no fabricated estimate or endless searching |
| Imperfect founder payment recollection presented as verified first-customer price | Fail calibration; preserve attribution, uncertainty and milestone distinctions |
| Concrete operating constraint, choice and execution explained; revenue impact unknown | May pass the episode with bounded claims, not certify the whole book |
| Every launch treated as a pivot or mutually exclusive period | Repair periodization; distinguish material changes and overlapping offers |
| Purported finding remains equally applicable after substituting another company name | Ground it in case evidence or present it as general context |

Use existing review records or one focused skill-evaluation report. Disclose same-investigator judgments; neither a predetermined expected result nor a static keyword check is an observed behavioral pass. If a case fails, revise the instructions and repeat affected evaluation, preserving failures and reasons.

Record which checks were actually performed and their limits. Frontmatter/link checks alone do not demonstrate runtime research quality. Repair demonstrated findings and repeat affected checks. Packaging/ZIP validation, installation or publication are performed only when those actions are requested; a local authorized edit does not need a release ceremony.
