# Internal readiness and final user review

[Strategy](strategy-and-current-state.md) · [Synthesis](research-synthesis-and-lessons.md) · [Dossier](../templates/project-completion-dossier.md)

## Stage progression

Internal stage checks verify covered questions, source integrity, major-conclusion RT work, dependencies, limits and durable records. They do not require another human acceptance or a fresh phase contract approval when covered by the consolidated agreement. Refine contracts internally and continue. Combine stage dossiers where that improves clarity instead of producing ceremonial files.

## Unknowns versus missing work

Use the agreed intake criteria. An important investigated unknown may receive a qualified disposition with attempted avenues, why further available work has low expected value, impact, limitations and revisit triggers; importance need not be downgraded. This is not evidence resolution. Keep claim confidence/review verdict separate from execution readiness.

Unperformed required investigation/review, missed material current offers, broken deliverables, unrecovered state or an explicitly user-defined must-have answer still blocks readiness. Do not use blanket “qualified” labels to skip work. A single inaccessible invoice does not automatically block the whole study unless the agreed objective makes it indispensable. Budget exhaustion is not evidence exhaustion. Ask a new decision only if the agreement cannot resolve a true blocker; continue unaffected work.

## Final project audit

Before delivery verify:
1. Each agreed strategic question and the full-company GTM cycle is addressed, or explicitly investigated/qualified under agreed criteria.
2. Material historical changes and current/emerging product/buyer portfolio are reconciled at the cutoff (or the explicit historical-only boundary).
3. Major changes explain choices, alternatives, trade-offs, execution, commercial mechanisms and outcomes/unknowns; mentions alone fail.
4. Major conclusions have substantive adversarial searches/adjudication; causal and future claims remain calibrated.
5. The overview and adaptive deep dives pass coverage, strategic-depth, evidence and reader/editorial reviews.
6. Evidence IDs/lineage, relevant revisions/dependencies, budget consumption, links/build where applicable and recovery state are consistent.
7. No remaining actual authority/limit blocker defeats the objective.

Persist a criterion-based dossier with scope, actual work, evidence/review links, limits, verdict, remaining unknowns and validation actually performed. Do not call static checks runtime proof or fabricate a reviewer. Update navigation/journal and checkpoint last.

## Delivery and goal boundary

Use `ready-for-user-review` or `ready-for-user-review-qualified` after internal checks; keep any true blocker explicit. Deliver the book, strategic findings, material unknowns, actual validation and consumption, then **stop autonomous research**. A goal explicitly scoped to producing this review-ready deliverable may be completed at delivery; that does not mean user acceptance. Do not set a goal paused without a user request or use blocked before the tool's applicable threshold.

User acceptance is `pending` until actual feedback/approval. Offer later step-by-step chapter review; a user request to review/revise covered work resumes that work without renewed intake/phase permissions. After delivery, encountered evidence may be preserved and affected findings flagged, but reopening alone does not resume research. Apply the [post-delivery reopening boundary](dependency-impact-and-reopening.md#4-completed-project-reopening), including any explicit authorization extending execution beyond delivery. Preserve old versions and existing spending/scope limits. Publishing, installation or external messaging is not implied by research completion.
