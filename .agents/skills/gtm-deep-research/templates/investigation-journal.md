## Journal maintenance rules

- Append meaningful developments in order; summarize repeated low-value searches rather than logging every tool invocation.
- Preserve consequential corrections or retractions instead of overwriting history without explanation.
- Never confuse a proposed source to inspect with a source actually inspected.
- Record substantive sources/claims/concepts first, then update indexes and journal references, validate, and advance the checkpoint last.
- Refer to an explicit source register for provenance and a decision ledger for approvals. Journal text is not a substitute.
- A major conclusion review remains `pending` until an actual independent [independent red-team pass](../references/red-team-and-adjudication.md) is recorded; journal `RT` references and actual outcomes without assuming approval.
