## One approval, autonomous refinement

One actual approval covers the consolidated charter, full plan and covered internal contracts. Derive/refine stage questions internally; no routine phase/chapter permission gates. New findings that change the answer, analytical framing or outline within the objective are normal research. Ask only for essential input, true subject/objective expansion, hard-limit changes, paid access/outreach or uncovered external actions. Continue unaffected work. Preserve existing project approvals and caps; do not silently migrate them.
