## Intake and scope

Assess evidence complexity, not answer length. Perform bounded reconnaissance to ask informed questions. Use thirteen discovery dimensions internally, reuse existing answers and grill only consequential ambiguity upfront. Clarify strategy questions, audience, cutoff, current/emerging coverage, constraints, allowed unknowns and review expectations. Present one execution agreement covering the full plan/internal contracts, workspace and explicit goal-start authorization. Unknown company facts belong to research, not a questionnaire the user must answer.
