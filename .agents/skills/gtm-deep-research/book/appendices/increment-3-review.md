## Mandatory boundaries that remain

- **No red-team review execution or adjudication.** Major conclusions retain `review=pending`; no self-issued passed verdicts.
- **No fully operational budget-management/extension procedure.** Honor known approvals and hard ceilings; stop if usage cannot be reliably kept within a hard ceiling.
- **No full subphase/project completion dossier or autonomous completion.** Closure of a genuinely exhausted **noncritical gap** is not closure of its subphase.
- **No final Docusaurus-style research-book generation, final lessons or learning checkpoint system.** Provisional `K` concepts support later synthesis.
- **No completed-project reopening/automatic cross-project invalidation service.** Preserve dependency references, but later lifecycle implementation is pending.
- **No live research, installation, paid source access or real workspace initialization** as part of this implementation increment.

## Review method

The accompanying `gtm-deep-research-increment-3-static-review.txt` reports static file checks, link integrity, decision mapping, and ZIP integrity. It does **not** prove correct behavior during a live Codex research run. All future increments require applicable explicit approval.
