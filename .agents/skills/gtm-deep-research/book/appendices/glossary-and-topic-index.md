This index supports quick navigation through the fourteen-chapter specification. Terms are **operational conventions**, not proof that any company-research evidence exists.

## A–C

| Term | Meaning | Read more |
|---|---|---|
| **Approval gate** | An explicit user authorization for a consequential charter, plan, spending, workspace or release decision | [Chapter 02](../part-01-foundations/02-governance-and-planning.md) |
| **Charter** | Versioned approved research objective, boundaries, evidence standards and completion criteria | [Chapter 01](../part-01-foundations/01-research-modes-and-charters.md) |
| **CLM** | Project-scoped claim record with support, contradictions, confidence and evidence/review status | [Chapter 04](../part-02-investigation/04-evidence-and-source-integrity.md) |
| **Concept / K** | Workspace-wide semantic knowledge object with stable `K-...` identity | [Chapter 08](../part-03-okf-architecture/08-research-records-and-identity.md) |
| **Confidence** | High/medium/low/unverified qualitative evidence judgment with rationale | [Chapter 04](../part-02-investigation/04-evidence-and-source-integrity.md) |

## D–I

| Term | Meaning | Read more |
