## Real limits without arbitrary source quotas

Record user-assigned hard ceilings and useful actual usage as measured, estimated with basis or unavailable. Unassigned dimensions stay unassigned; do not invent per-phase caps or extension ceremonies. Existing authorized caps remain binding and cannot be pooled or bypassed by renaming stages. No budget omission authorizes spending. Consume only actual preauthorized extensions within every ceiling; ask before exceeding a real limit or adding external commitments. Coverage and information gain guide effort; quota exhaustion proves neither evidence exhaustion nor readiness.
