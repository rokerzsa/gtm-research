## Final user review

Capture learning preferences upfront. During autonomous research, updates are concise and nonblocking; defer deep teaching by default. Internal chapter reviews do not require user signoff. Deliver the internally checked book ready for user review, then stop research. The user may review the overview and deeper chapters step by step afterward. Do not infer learner responses or acceptance, conflate agreement with factual certainty or personalize lessons without a separate request.
