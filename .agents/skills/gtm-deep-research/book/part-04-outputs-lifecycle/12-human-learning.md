# Chapter 12 — Human Learning

[Book home](../index.md) · [Part IV](index.md) · [Previous: Chapter 11](11-book-architecture-and-navigation.md) · [Next: Chapter 13](13-completion-and-acceptance.md)

**Status:** Approved consolidated specification, adaptive learning procedure available. **Governing decision:** DR-035. See [human-learning procedure](../../references/human-learning.md) and [optional learning checkpoint template](../../templates/learning-checkpoint.md).

**Purpose:** Help a human understand important GTM findings, challenge causal accounts and reason about transferable implications without conflating learning performance with research evidence or human authorization.

## 12.1 Learning is a distinct research goal

Deep Research supports independent investigation and collaborative learning in adjustable proportions. The charter should express the intended learning depth, existing human knowledge, preferred interaction and any explicit human gates. Research can remain evidence-grounded regardless of whether a learner agrees with the finding.

The skill must teach what evidence *does and does not* establish rather than merely deliver a polished report. A human should be able to inspect and challenge central conclusions through linked claims, sources and competing causal models.

## 12.2 Adaptive checkpoints at consequential moments

Offer checkpoints at meaningful discoveries, competing historical or GTM mechanisms, major red-team disagreements, phase boundaries or transferable lessons. Avoid rote quizzes after every source or section; checkpoint frequency and depth are configurable. The purpose is reasoning, not ceremonial recall.

A checkpoint can explain that one interview establishes a founder *reported* an early-customer milestone while the underlying number of paying customers remains unverified; the learner can then consider what invoices, contracts, dates or independent customer testimony would discriminate the competing accounts.

## 12.3 Targeted learning interaction

At a useful checkpoint, Codex should:

1. Briefly explain the important finding and why it matters for approved research questions.
2. Distinguish direct evidence, reports and hypothesis; cite current `K`, `CLM`, `SRC` and material `RT` records.
3. Compare one or two consequential alternatives or confounders.
4. Ask a targeted question requiring analysis, e.g. *What evidence would distinguish developer community adoption from enterprise recruiting demand as the main acquisition driver?*
5. Discuss the human's reasoning respectfully, identify overlooked assumptions and explain relevant counterevidence.
6. Explain what the researched case teaches and which general business conditions limit the lesson. Apply it to the learner's business only when separately requested; never import that business from conversation history.
7. Summarize optional follow-up reading/questions without treating a learner response as factual approval.

Questions should adapt to the human's stated sophistication and learning priorities, without becoming mandatory mastery exams by default.

## 12.4 Three separate authorities

**Understanding:** The learner can explain or critique the model.

**Evidence evaluation:** Authoritative source/claim records and required red-team reviews determine evidentiary support and confidence.

**Governance authorization:** An explicit human decision approves charter, plan, scope or budgets where required.

A learner's agreement does not cause a `CLM` evidence state or `RT` review outcome to change. A demonstrated understanding of a GTM tactic does not grant permission for paid access, extra research budget, a new plan or a charter amendment.

## 12.5 Nonblocking learning

Pending learning discussions are **nonblocking for unrelated authorized work**. A checkpoint becomes blocking only where the charter or another applicable human decision gate explicitly requires a response; record the exact approval requirement rather than inferring one. Continue unrelated work when safe and budget-authorized.

Learning checkpoint notes are optional, compact and traceable to actual findings and questions. Do not fabricate human answers, preserve unnecessary sensitive personal assessments or create a new global ID scheme for teaching notes.

## 12.6 Learning continuity

When useful, store topics discussed, meaningful interpretive disagreements, what evidence would resolve them, and optional future reading in an [optional checkpoint note](../../templates/learning-checkpoint.md). These are educational records, not substitutes for underlying research evidence. Notes follow the same standalone OKF ordered-write and authorization rules if persisted.

## 12.7 Increment boundary

Interactive learning and draft synthesis are available during authorized research. [Criterion-based completion](../../references/completion-and-acceptance.md) and [impact-based reopening](../../references/dependency-impact-and-reopening.md) are separate procedures, not learning approval gates. A learning checkpoint itself is **not** project completion and does not establish book correctness.
