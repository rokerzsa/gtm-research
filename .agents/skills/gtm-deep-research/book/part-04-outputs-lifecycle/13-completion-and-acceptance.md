## Readiness is separate from acceptance

Audit agreed questions, whole GTM coverage, material current/emerging direction, strategic depth, adversarial evidence and reader quality before delivery. An important investigated unknown can be explicitly qualified under agreed criteria without lowering its importance. Missing required research/review, a user-defined indispensable answer, broken artifacts or unrecovered state still blocks readiness. Covered phases progress autonomously. Persist a criterion-based dossier and checkpoint last; deliver ready-for-user-review (qualified where needed), stop research and keep user acceptance pending. A goal scoped to that deliverable may complete without claiming acceptance.
